Privacy Policy for Dokany (دكاني)

Last updated: May 20, 2024

This Privacy Policy applies to the Dokany (دكاني) mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), created as an Installment and Debt Management application for local store owners and individuals.

1. Information Collection and Use

We value your privacy. Dokany is designed to store all your data locally on your mobile device. We do not transmit, collect, or store your financial records, customer data, or personal information on any external servers.

2. Permissions Requested

To provide core functionalities, the Application may request the following device permissions:

  • READ_CONTACTS: Used strictly to allow you to easily select customer phone numbers and names from your contacts list when adding a new installment or debt record. We do not upload or share your contacts with anyone.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to deliver timely payment reminders and notifications regarding upcoming or overdue installments and debts.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to automatically reschedule your active payment reminders after your device restarts.

3. Data Storage and Backups

All data entered into Dokany (such as debts, installments, customer names, and transaction amounts) remains strictly on your device. You have full ownership and control over your data, including exporting local PDF reports or creating local backup files.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application does not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services that collect personally identifiable information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, deleting data within the app or uninstalling the Application will permanently erase all stored records and settings. You can clear all app data anytime via your Android system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.
